Frequently asked questions

Does Android need my Windows PC running?

Local character management is designed to work independently. Features that rely on external data or server-specific imports may have additional requirements while development continues.

Can I use older D&D content?

The app tracks source and rules edition so legacy and revised content can be distinguished. Rules automation is still being expanded.

Is my data backed up?

Local saving and backup tools are part of the project. Export a backup before reinstalling, resetting app storage, or switching devices.
